# ArrowNook: Logic Puzzle Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

ArrowNook: Logic Puzzle is developed by Anupam Pradhan, India.

## Data Stored On Your Device

ArrowNook stores game progress, coin balance, sound preference and haptic preference locally on your device. The game does not include an account system and does not upload puzzle progress to an ArrowNook server. You can remove this local data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

## Ads And Consent

ArrowNook uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to show ads and manage consent where required. Google may process device identifiers, advertising ID, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information and ad interaction data to provide, limit, measure and secure ads.

ArrowNook requests non-personalized ads by default and limits ad content to a general-audience rating. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing by Google and its partners.

Google privacy information:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903

## Google Play Features

ArrowNook may use Google Play services to show an in-app review prompt and to offer an
in-app update when a newer version is available. These flows are provided by Google Play and
are subject to Google's privacy policy.

## Data Sharing

ArrowNook does not sell personal data. Data processed by Google Mobile Ads or Google Play
features is handled by Google and its partners according to Google's policies and the consent
choices available in your region.

## Children

ArrowNook is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not designed for children under 13.

## Security

Data transmitted by Google SDKs is sent using modern transport security such as HTTPS.

## Contact

Use the developer support email shown on the Google Play listing for privacy questions or deletion requests. ArrowNook has no account system or server-side player profile, so uninstalling the app or clearing app storage removes locally stored game data from your device.
